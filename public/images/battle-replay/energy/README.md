# Energy type symbols

Official vector marks and base colours extracted from page 4 (Energy Types) of the Pokémon Trading Card Game Paradox Rift rulebook:
https://assets.pokemon.com/assets/cms2/pdf/trading-card-game/rulebook/par_rulebook_en.pdf

The SVG paths and fills are preserved from the source PDF. Gloss, shadow, and attachment animation are presentation styling added by Poke Notes, not extracted TCG Live artwork. Pokémon artwork and marks belong to their respective owners.
