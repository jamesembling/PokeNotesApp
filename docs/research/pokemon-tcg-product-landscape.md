# Pokémon TCG product landscape

Snapshot: 24 August 2026. This is a broad practical scan of current, publicly discoverable product-owned websites, official documentation, and app-store listings across six jobs: battle-log review, personal match journaling, deck/strategy management, tournament capture, public meta analysis, and active playtesting. It cannot prove that every private spreadsheet, Discord bot, abandoned project, or unindexed app has been found. Capabilities below are claims or visibly documented features from those sources, not an independent quality or data-accuracy audit.

## Scope and classification

- **Direct competitor:** a player-facing workspace that materially overlaps personal deck, match, tournament, matchup, or improvement tracking.
- **Adjacent:** overlaps one important job—playing, coaching, public meta analysis, community content, or collection—but not the whole personal practice record.
- **Infrastructure:** organizer, official data, event, or API systems that another player product could depend on.

**Reference baseline — [Training Court](https://www.trainingcourt.app/about):** a web app for importing Pokémon TCG Live logs, displaying turns, and persisting tournament rounds, opposing decks, and individual game records to an account. Its own description presents deeper practice analytics as future work, so it is a useful baseline for log-to-review and event-history workflows rather than a complete strategy/collection platform.

## What the scan shows

1. **The closest direct overlap is fragmented.** SixPrizer is strongest on imported logs, private testing data, deck-version comparison, and next-test guidance. PokeTrainer TCG is strongest on explicit matchup plans, prize maps, sequencing, and error tracking. PokeDuel and PokéMatch cover the lighter “log games and see win rates” end of the spectrum.
2. **Limitless and Trainer Hill own the public competitive-data layer.** Limitless combines public decklists, tournament history, a deck builder, online tournaments, and a documented API for placings, decklists, and matches ([developer guide](https://docs.limitlesstcg.com/developer.html)); Trainer Hill turns tournament data into matchup, card, evolution, and bracket analysis. Their cited pages do not evidence a full private player journal with durable strategy notes and historical personal records.
3. **Tournament authority and personal reflection are separate systems.** Play! Tools and RK9 Labs handle sanctioned/event operations; Training Court, SixPrizer, and similar products handle player-owned review. This separation matters when evaluating whether an app is a source of truth for standings/decklists or merely a player’s interpretation of a match.
4. **Collection products are another silo.** TCG Collector, Collectr, and TCGplayer offer strong inventory, scanning, valuation, and marketplace workflows. In this scan, no collection product also demonstrated the full chain from owned cards to versioned competitive decklists, matchup plans, imported battle evidence, and tournament history.
5. **A practical integration surface is visible.** PTCG Live generates the battle logs; Limitless exposes tournament/deck/match data and documents an API; official Pokémon pages expose card and event references; RK9 and Play! Tools operate events. These are complementary data sources rather than interchangeable products.

6. **The market is moving toward “decision support,” not just record storage.** SixPrizer’s next-test guidance, PokeTrainer’s error/matchup planning, poko’s alternate-line analysis, and TheDipidis’ meta-call/testing-group tooling all try to turn a stored result into a next action. That is the strategic bar PokeNotes should benchmark against.

## Scope boundary

Pokémon TCG Pocket products were not treated as direct competitors because Pocket is a separate rules/product environment. [Pocket-Tracker](https://pocket-tracker.com/app) and [Pokémon Meta](https://www.pokemonmeta.com/) are still useful adjacent references for match logging, ranked progression, matchup dashboards, deck prediction, and community meta UX if Pocket is later included in scope.
