## Design Context

### Users
Poke Notes is used by competitive Pokemon TCG players who need a fast, reliable workspace for reviewing battle logs, deck strategy, and probability decisions. They are using the app to make better play and deck-building decisions, often on mobile as well as desktop.

### Brand Personality
Serious, focused, and professional. The interface should feel closer to a sports analytics dashboard than a fan site. It should be calm, clear, and trustworthy without feeling sterile.

### Aesthetic Direction
Data-first, consistency-led UI. Shared layouts, shared card treatments, restrained color, flat purposeful surfaces, and strong light/dark parity. Avoid decorative hero treatments, gradients, glass effects, or page-specific visual experiments.

### Design Principles
- Data first: numbers, records, and outcomes are the primary content.
- Consistent over clever: pages should reuse the same layout and component patterns.
- Legible in both modes: every surface and text treatment must work in light and dark themes.
- Mobile is real: responsive behavior should be designed, not bolted on.
- Function before flourish: visual cleanup must not compromise workflows or reliability.
